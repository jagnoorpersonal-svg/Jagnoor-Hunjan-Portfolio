Mazingira
Environmental Justice
Offline-First GPS Reporting Photo Upload Maps English · Swahili Sync on Connect Community-Driven
A community-driven Environmental Justice Tracker that puts pollution reporting in the hands of the people most affected by it. Residents in informal settlements, areas near schools, and industrial zones can document air pollution, illegal dumping, factory emissions, water contamination, and noise pollution — with photos and precise GPS coordinates — directly from their phones.

The app works fully offline, queuing reports locally and syncing to the backend the moment connectivity returns — so a weak signal never stops a report from being filed. An integrated map layer makes environmental disparities visible across vulnerable communities, turning lived experience into documented evidence. Available in both English and Swahili.
◎ Proposal
Field Monitor
M&E Field Tool
Field Reporting Real-Time Sync Encrypted DB Smartphone-First M&E Tools Instant Updates
A proposed field monitoring app designed for the M&E team — built around how field officers actually work. Instead of paper forms or slow desktop dashboards, staff log tasks, activities, and progress directly from their smartphones in real time.

Reports update instantly across the team, giving programme managers live visibility without chasing emails. Data is stored in a secure, encrypted database, keeping sensitive field information protected. Designed to be fast, intuitive, and friction-free — because in the field, every minute counts.
